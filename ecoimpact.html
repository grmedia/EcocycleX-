Data integrity notice
EcoImpact reports only what has been recorded and verified. Environmental conversion figures (like emissions avoided) are always estimates based on a stated methodology — never presented as directly measured facts. Verified means a person has reviewed and approved the evidence behind a record.

Quick Actions

Impact Activities

Material Recovery & Diversion

Double-counting protection active. A single collection event, weighing, or transaction can only be counted once. Manual entries are labelled as manual and kept separate from sensor-verified measurements. Source references link each record back to its origin.

Reuse, Repair & Product-Life Extension

Reused and repaired items are counted by activity and item count. Environmental savings are only calculated when an approved, documented methodology exists. We never convert every reused item into a fixed environmental benefit without justification.

Community & Social Impact

Participant privacy. Personal information about participants and volunteers is never published without consent. Numbers shown here are aggregates only. Definitions of "participant", "volunteer", and "beneficiary" must be documented by the organisation.

Impact Projects

Goals & Targets

A goal is only marked achieved when underlying verified data meets the defined target. Progress shown here comes from verified source records.

Calculation Methods

No universal conversion factors are embedded. Every calculation method must be defined, documented, and versioned here — with source, assumptions, geographic limitations, and uncertainty. Environmental claims can only be calculated once a method is approved.

Impact Indicators

Indicators show measured values (from real activity counts and weights), calculated values (using an approved method), and estimated values (with uncertainty). Each card labels its type and links to its source records.

Verification Centre

A record is only verified after an authorised verifier reviews the evidence and approves it. Submitting a record does not make it verified. Verification badges appear only after genuine review.

Data Quality & Integrity

Suspicious records are flagged for review — never silently deleted or edited. Original values are preserved with full correction history. Duplicate detection prevents double-counting across imports and sources.

Evidence Centre

Evidence supports verification. Real file uploads require a genuine secure storage integration — integration pending. For now, evidence is recorded as a reference (source, description, identifier) and labelled appropriately.

Reports

No certification claims. EcoImpact does not produce carbon credits, sustainability certifications, or government compliance documents unless an external authority is genuinely connected. Reports are internal summaries for the reporting organisation.

Impact Stories

Stories turn verified records into understandable summaries. Human-authored stories are labelled as Author narrative. AI-assisted text is labelled AI-assisted and grounded in real data only — the AI never invents achievements.

Help & Definitions

What is EcoImpact?

EcoImpact measures the real impact of circular-economy activities across EcoCycle X. It connects recorded activities to traceable measurements, transparent calculations, and responsible reporting.

Central principle: No evidence, no verification. No verified data, no confirmed impact claim.

Calculation methods

Environmental conversion factors (e.g., "X kg of plastic = Y kg CO₂e avoided") are never embedded in EcoImpact. Every method you use must be:

DocumentedWith source and reference
VersionedEvery update creates a new version
ScopedWith geographic and material limitations
Uncertainty-awareStated precision and assumptions
ApprovedBy an authorised reviewer

If no method exists for a metric, EcoImpact displays the underlying activity counts and states that the conversion is not configured.

Production readiness

EcoImpact is not yet approved for public launch. Before public claims can be made, the following must be completed: authentication, database rules, source integrations, calculation methods, verification workflows, privacy controls, security, reporting, testing, monitoring, and deployment configuration.
