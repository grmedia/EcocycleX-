What EcoConnect Does

EcoConnect is the circular-economy network of EcoCycle X. It connects people, businesses, collectors, recyclers, artisans, schools, NGOs and EcoHubs — so materials find their way to the right hands.

How to use it
1. Complete your profileVerification boosts trust
2. Post material needs/offersBroadcast what you have or need
3. Discover membersFilter by role and location
4. ConnectSend requests, build trust
