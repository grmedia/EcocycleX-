Smart Bin Monitoring

Fill levels shown are only from genuine device readings. Offline bins display their last known reading with the timestamp and a clear stale label. A bin is never labelled "full" or "nearly full" without a valid reading.

Weighing & Material Measurement

Every weight record stores device ID, material category, value, unit, timestamp, operator, calibration status and source. Manually entered weights are labelled as manual and never merged with sensor-verified readings. Uncalibrated readings are flagged for review.

Environmental Monitoring

Environmental readings are monitoring information only. They are not medical, public-health, or safety certifications unless separately validated by the relevant authority. Safe-range alerts are informational — not compliance statements.

GPS & Location Monitoring

Location privacy. Precise locations are only shown when a device is legitimately GPS-enabled, the user has explicit permission, and privacy consent is enabled. Locations are distinguished as live, last-known, approximate area, or unavailable. Precise positions of homes, schools, or vulnerable sites are never exposed without consent.

Device Health & Maintenance

A device is not shown as healthy merely because it is registered. Health is derived from genuine signals — last communication, calibration status, sensor errors, battery, tamper indicators, and maintenance history.

Automation & Response Rules

Automation rules never create financial commitments, dispatch vehicles, or change device configuration without explicit manual authorisation. High-impact actions require a confirmation step. Every rule has a genuine trigger, condition, action, owner, and full execution history.

What is EcoSmart?

EcoSmart connects real physical devices — smart bins, digital scales, environmental sensors, GPS equipment, and IoT gateways — to EcoCycle X. It gives users and organisations the ability to monitor, understand, and respond to genuine signals from the physical world.

Core principle: No verified device data, no claim of live intelligence.
