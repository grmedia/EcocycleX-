What is EcoWallet?

What EcoWallet is

EcoWallet is the financial, rewards, and value-management module of EcoCycle X. It records genuine payments, collection fees, seller earnings, refunds, deposits, rewards, EcoPoints, and approved incentives — all linked to real activity in EcoMarket, EcoCollect, and EcoConnect.

It operates currently as a secure transaction ledger and payment-orchestration interface. This means it is the place where your financial activity is recorded, ordered, and made visible — not a bank account, and not a stored-value wallet.

Production readiness

EcoWallet is not yet approved for public financial use. Before any real money moves, the following must be completed: payment provider integration, legal and regulatory review, identity verification, security testing, fraud controls, privacy policy, terms of use, refund policy, support process, financial reconciliation, backups, monitoring, real-time infrastructure, and real-world payment tests.

Until then, every button that would imply a real financial result is intentionally disabled or clearly labelled.
