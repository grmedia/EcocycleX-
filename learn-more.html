WHAT IS ECOCYCLE X?

EcoCycle X is not just a recycling app — it is a digital circular‑economy ecosystem. It helps you identify waste, understand what can be done with it, discover its potential value, connect with buyers and collectors, request collections, earn from recoverable materials, learn about sustainability, and track your environmental contribution.

THE 9 ECOCYCLE X SYSTEMS

Each system is a powerful tool in your circular-economy toolkit.

1. EcoScan AI

AI waste scanning. Photograph any item to identify material, category, recyclability, reusability, repairability, estimated weight, value, and recommended action.

2. EcoMarket

Buy, sell, and trade recyclable materials. AI helps create listings and matches materials with potential buyers, recyclers, or manufacturers.

3. EcoCollect

Request pickups from verified collectors and track collection status. Optimise routes with EcoRoute AI.

4. EcoWallet

Secure digital value centre. Track verified sales, payments, rewards, EcoPoints, and transaction history. Future-ready for payment integrations.

5. EcoConnect

Network and map connecting households, schools, businesses, collectors, recyclers, repairers, EcoHubs, and environmental projects.

6. EcoGenius

AI assistant for recycling guidance, reuse and upcycling ideas, repair suggestions, and green-entrepreneurship information.

7. EcoSmart

Connects smart bins, digital scales, fill-level sensors, GPS devices, and vehicles to EcoCycle X for real-time monitoring and automation.

8. EcoImpact

Combines EcoPoints, challenges, leaderboards, recycling history, and environmental-impact tracking.

9. EcoCommand

Advanced analytics and environmental-intelligence centre for waste hotspots, collection patterns, and trends.

AI cannot guarantee exact weight from a camera.

Until a connected scale or verified weighing station provides actual weight, camera-based estimates are labelled AI ESTIMATE.
